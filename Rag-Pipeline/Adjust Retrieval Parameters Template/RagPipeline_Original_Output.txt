QUERY: What causes database connection timeouts?
================================================================================

Retrieved 3 relevant tickets:

  [1] TICK-002: Database connection timeout in production

  [2] TICK-021: Database connection excedding in production

  [3] TICK-010: Dashboard loading extremely slowly

Generating answer...

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Database connection timeouts can be caused by issues related to the connection pool being either too small or too large for the application's peak load. 

1. **Ticket ID: TICK-002** - In this case, the database connection pool was sized too small, leading to 'connection pool exhausted' errors and timeouts during peak hours. The resolution involved increasing the maximum connections from 20 to 100 and optimizing long-running queries that were holding connections.

2. **Ticket ID: TICK-021** - Conversely, this ticket indicated that the connection pool was sized too large, which also resulted in similar errors. The resolution included decreasing the minimum connections from 20 to 10 and terminating non-running queries that were holding connections.

Both scenarios highlight the importance of properly sizing the database connection pool to match the application's load and managing long-running queries effectively.

QUERY: Why are emails not being delivered?
================================================================================

  [2] TICK-022: Email notifications not being already registered

  [3] TICK-013: Webhook deliveries failing intermittently

Generating answer...

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Emails are not being delivered due to the sending IP being blacklisted by the email service provider because of spam complaints. This issue was documented in Ticket ID: TICK-006, where users reported not receiving password reset and confirmation emails despite SMTP server logs indicating successful sends. The resolution involved implementing SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records, switching to a dedicated IP address with a proper warmup process, and adding email deliverability monitoring.

Additionally, there was another issue in Ticket ID: TICK-022 where users were unable to register with their email because the email was already registered. To resolve this, users needed to delete their existing account before re-registering. 

These two tickets highlight different aspects of email delivery issues.
