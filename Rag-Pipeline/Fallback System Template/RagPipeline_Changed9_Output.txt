QUERY: What causes database connection timeouts?
================================================================================

Retrieved 3 relevant tickets:

  [1] TICK-002: Database connection timeout in production

  [2] TICK-021: Database connection excedding in production

  [3] TICK-010: Dashboard loading extremely slowly

Generating answer...

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Database connection timeouts can be caused by issues related to the connection pool configuration and long-running queries. 

1. **Connection Pool Exhaustion**: In Ticket ID TICK-002, the application experienced intermittent 500 errors due to the connection pool being sized too small for peak load, leading to 'connection pool exhausted' errors. The resolution involved increasing the maximum connections from 20 to 100 and optimizing long-running queries that were holding connections.

2. **Improper Pool Sizing**: Conversely, in Ticket ID TICK-021, the application also faced similar intermittent 500 errors, but in this case, the connection pool was sized too large for the peak load. The resolution included decreasing the minimum connections from 20 to 10 and terminating not-running queries that were holding connections.

Both tickets highlight that connection pool configuration and the management of long-running queries are critical factors in preventing database connection timeouts.

QUERY: Why are emails not being delivered?
================================================================================

Retrieved 3 relevant tickets:

  [1] TICK-006: Email notifications not being delivered

  [2] TICK-022: Email notifications not being already registered

  [3] TICK-013: Webhook deliveries failing intermittently

Generating answer...

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Emails are not being delivered due to the sending IP being blacklisted by the email service provider because of spam complaints. This issue was documented in Ticket ID: TICK-006, where users reported not receiving password reset and confirmation emails despite SMTP server logs indicating successful sends. The resolution involved implementing SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records, switching to a dedicated IP address with a proper warmup process, and adding email deliverability monitoring.

Additionally, there was another issue in Ticket ID: TICK-022 where users were unable to register with their email because the email was already registered. In this case, users needed to delete their existing account to re-register. 

If you are experiencing email delivery issues, it may be related to either of these situations.
